# M5 — Video campaigns

- Storyboard: director-written storyboard (structure, aspect, music mood, end card, frames with duration/size/angle/move/lens/aperture/focus/action/transitions/overlays/hints/lighting), running-time bar against the target, drag reorder (synced to keyframe rows), frame drawer editing synced into shot specs, revisions with restore.
- Keyframes → clips: approve frames, generate keyframes, pick one per frame in Review, generate clips (i2v / r2v / first-last-frame / product presets chosen automatically or per shot), clip review.
- Assemble: timeline editor (ordered clips with in/out, speed, fit, transitions, text overlays), music track with offset/volume/fades/mode, end card with logo, output channel/dimensions/fps, preview and final renders with players, revisions.
- Exports reuse the stills flow for the final render and clips.
